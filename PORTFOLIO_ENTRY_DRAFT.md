## Text Provenance Lab

A transparent evidence lab for text watermarking. The [live historical replay](https://text-provenance-lab.vercel.app) lets visitors inspect hash-verified Kirchenbauer detector scores across two models, eight length cells, and recorded edit conditions with fixed scales and explicit denominators. A clearly separate mechanics toy explains why local statistical evidence changes under editing. The repository also contains a frozen, unexecuted protocol for a future Kirchenbauer–SynthID comparison.

**Release state:** Historical coursework replay in production. Do not describe it as a completed SynthID evaluation. Scientific promotion of the planned comparison still requires confirmatory caches, fresh H1 reproduction, and independent scientific/compliance-framing review.
