03 · Frozen future protocol · not yet executed

The planned comparison chooses thresholds before testing.

1

Calibrate

Three unwatermarked completions per calibration prompt define each detector's threshold. Prompt-clustered bootstrap intervals keep those triplets together.

2

Hold out

Matched control, Kirchenbauer, and SynthID generations use the same prompt seed. Held-out outputs never select the threshold.

3

Attack

Thirty-three fixed conditions cover edits, partial text, two paraphrasers, two translation pivots, and regenerate-from-summary laundering.

4

Report either way

TPR, realized FPR, uncertainty, failures, and quality all ship. The measurement informs policy discussion; it does not pronounce legal compliance.

Scope

The completed replay covers two English-language models and one Kirchenbauer-style scheme. The planned study would test an open-source SynthID implementation—not Google's production deployment—with author-run attacks and hundreds, not tens of thousands, of calibration controls. No legal advice.
